Both bent = ✊ fist, sound on. Both straight = 🖐️ open hand, sound off. Index straight + middle bent = 🤚 next instrument. Hold a posture for ~0.35 s: that delay is what stops a volume swell firing them by accident.
Voice & modes
Drag the pad = roll (brightness) and tilt (pitch). space wrist flick, n next scene, c recalibrate flex, j reverse, k pitch-only, v record, o overdub, p loop, g granular, b slices, m drone, l live voice, 1–7 instruments, h switch hand. Live voice: wear headphones, speak, roll = pitch, motion = echo.
Song — play a record with the loop hand
——
Whatever loads here replaces the loop buffer, so every loop-hand gesture works on it: roll scrubs, tilt stretches 0.5×–2×, a fist freezes, a flick fires the slice under your hand. Add #t=60 to a link, or #60 to a path, to start 60 s in — scrubbing a whole track means one wrist rotation covers four minutes. A .mid is different: it is notes, not a recording, so it plays through your own instruments. Tilt then transposes the tune instead of picking a note, the loop hand pulls the tempo, and the posture that changes instrument changes what is playing it. Loading a record mutes the synth, so your hands shape the record rather than playing over it — m brings the instrument back if you want to solo on top. Eject stops it and empties the buffer; p just pauses. Reloading this page does not stop anything — the sound comes from the python process, and this is only a window onto it.
